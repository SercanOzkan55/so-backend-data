About

I learn complex systems by making their boundaries, evidence, and failure modes visible.

I'm Sercan Özkan, a computer engineering student and software developer building AI-assisted products, simulation systems, backend services, and immersive learning tools.

My projects move between web platforms, deterministic engines, data pipelines, and VR interaction. I want to trace how a decision is made, understand where data moves, and know what fails before adding another layer. Outside personal work I spent a summer on low-latency drone video streaming at Daşal Havacılık, measuring latency and packet loss across RTSP and RTP transports.

AI helps me plan, question, and document the work. I still check the source, run important paths, record uncertainty, and keep the technical decisions explainable.
